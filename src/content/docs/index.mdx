Saoi (Irish for a sage, said "see") is the product. Saoirse is the assistant inside it: a conversational
layer over simulated retail banking journeys for a fictional Demo Bank. The customer says what they
are trying to do. Saoirse works out the wider need, the customer chooses products, and facts are
collected once and reused across concurrent applications. The case persists across channels,
sessions, a partner and time.

## State of the project

This is a proof of concept, not a bank. Everything runs on synthetic Demo Bank data. Nothing real is
opened and no money moves. The code is open source under AGPL-3.0-only, and these docs are CC BY 4.0.
